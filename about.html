About

I'm Oliver, a Machine Learning Engineer & Researcher based in Charlotte, NC, with a dual focus on empirical research in MTL and production deployment within MTL as well as RL and post-training. I graduated with a B.S. in Computer Science from Appalachian State with a minor in Mathematics and a Data Science certificate. I'm now full-time as the sole ML Engineer at a stealth, pre-launch startup I joined after graduating. My research interests sit are in multi-task learning, reinforcement learning, and NLP.

I joined the startup orginally as an intern after my capstone project which was an ML harmonization system and real-time inference dashboard which became the initial production system for the startup and what I've been building onwards from, it's now much more comple than that. Today, I'm the sole MLE on our 4-person team, owning all ML architecture, DB, api, research, and production decisions regarding AI whithin our app and business
