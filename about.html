About

Research, engineering,
and learning by building.

I'm Oliver, a machine learning engineer and researcher based in Charlotte, North Carolina. My interests are in NLP, multi-task learning, post-training, and reinforcement learning. I graduated from Appalachian State with a B.S. in Computer Science, a minor in Mathematics, and a Data Science certificate.

I'm the sole ML engineer at a stealth, pre-launch startup, owning model development, evaluation, and integration. I keep the company's product and implementation details private.

I’m also the founder of LiminalML, a SaaS company I design, build, and run solo. Study and Studio are its tools for learning and explaining machine learning. On the side, my open-source project LexiMind explores shared representations and adaptation for language tasks, alongside a separate book-discovery app.

Coursework & self-study

A lot of my depth in ML, DL, and RL comes from deliberate self-study on top of my curriculum from university. I've worked through Stanford courses completely, watching full lectures, writing notes, doing problem sets, and exams.

Stanford (self-directed, lectures + exams + notes): CS229 Machine Learning · CS230 Deep Learning · CS336 Language Modeling from Scratch · CS224R Deep Reinforcement Learning · CME295/296 Transformers & LLMs.

Appalachian State: Applied Machine Learning · Advanced Reinforcement Learning · Artificial Intelligence · Computer Systems Numerical Methods · Computational Mathematics · Statistical Data Analysis · Linear Algebra · Theoretical Computer Science · Programming Languages (Functional Programming w/ Haskell) · Data Structures & Algorithms.

Other: DeepLearning.AI Machine Learning Specialization (Andrew Ng) — see certifications below.

Notable academic projects from university

Formal language interpreter with lambda calculus and type inference (Haskell) · pipelined CPU simulator (C/C++) · 2D game engine (Java). My senior capstone helped form the foundation for my full-time ML engineering work.
